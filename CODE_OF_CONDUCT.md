# Code of conduct

Many Hats is a small project and wants to stay a friendly one.

## Expected

- **Be kind and assume good intent.** Disagree about ideas, not people.
- **Help newcomers.** Every question is a fair one.
- **Keep it on topic.** Issues and pull requests are for Many Hats.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't post anyone's personal data, including your own account details in logs: "Collect Diagnostics" removes them for you.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes or language.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules.

**To report something**, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue. You can also reach the maintainer privately through a [security advisory](https://github.com/ryankkhalil19/many-hats/security/advisories/new) marked "conduct". Reports are kept confidential.
